Craft & Ethos

Meaning-making

Curiosity-driven stewardship of your game's capacity to produce meaning for the player. Appreciation for the smallest details in service to the fun and feel of your game.

Systems

Dialogue that branches inuitively and flows from UI constraints. Experienced in variable-driven state tracking, with quest logic that anticipates player creativity.

Voice

Vibrant character voices that access the senses, deliver meaningful information in undidactic ways, and give your game world an unmistakable vibe.

World-Building

Audience-aware lorekeeping. World depth that scales disproportionately to game size and complexity. Clear, structured, and thoughtful documentation that keeps game worlds consistent across a full production team.

Tone

Tone as an extension of the player fantasy first, building the right beats and voices around the core loop's unique features. Cozy to Grimdark & everything in between.

Tools

Eclectic experience with Twine, Ink, Scrivener, Gamemaker, Unreal Engine & Unity. Dialogue via plugins and custom scripting alike.

About

In short: I am a writer , recruiter, game dev, tenor, downhill demon hooper, DPS, educator, and baker based in San Francisco, CA.

My writing is musical, precise, and driven by the relentless twin pursuits of meaning and amusement. My first writerly practice was composing hip hop songs, which quickly transformed into a verse poetry fixation, then evolved into an obsession with fiction after reading Kurt Vonnegut’s “Cat’s Cradle”, and finally brought me to Game Dev after playing Hades. I have since written a historical fantasy novel, completed an MFA in Fiction, spent the past 4 years building games, and beat Elden Ring with a Rune Level 1 character.

In my free time, I bake pastries and walk around San Francisco with an iced matcha in hand. At present, I am training to dunk a basketball, and finally reading Ulysses (surely).
Thanks for reading!
